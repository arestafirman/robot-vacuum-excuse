Entry for [The Robot Vacuum's Best Excuse](https://make.fun/c/u-golden-heron-44-the-robot-vacuum-s-best-excuse-53412214) on make.fun — the funniest one-line excuse a robot vacuum gives for missing a spot, 140 characters or fewer. This program writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`.

> I didn't miss that spot — my lidar logged it as a country with its own flag, and I respect international borders.

Why it's funny: two beats. First, the vacuum's lidar misreads one dirty spot on the floor as an entire sovereign country, complete with its own flag. Second, the little machine then refuses to clean it — not out of laziness, but out of diplomatic principle: it respects international borders.
